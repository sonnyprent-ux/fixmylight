How FixMyLight works

  1. You report a broken traffic light. It takes about 30 seconds and you don’t need an account.
  2. We email the {{ cfg.CITY_NAME }}’s roads agency with the location, the fault and a map link.
  3. Every working day we send one polite reminder in the same email thread, saying how long it has been broken and how many people reported it.
  4. We stop as soon as anyone marks it fixed. After {{ cfg.MAX_FOLLOWUPS }} reminders we flag it for escalation to the ward councillor.

Why only one email a day?

A flooded inbox gets filtered. One clear, factual thread per light — with a growing count of affected people — is harder to ignore than hundreds of separate emails.

Your privacy

We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number. If you add your location, only the coordinates of the traffic light are stored and shared with the city.

Who runs this?

FixMyLight is free, open-source software. It is not run by the {{ cfg.CITY_NAME }}. For emergencies call 10111 or 112.
